# Sample AI Response Evaluation 2

## Task: Write a polite email to a client to reschedule a meeting from Monday to Wednesday

### AI Response A
"Hi Mr. Adebayo,  
I hope you are well. I need to reschedule our meeting from Monday to Wednesday at 10am due to a conflict. Please let me know if that time works for you.  
Thank you,  
Abimbola"

**Evaluation:**
- **Accuracy**: Correct. States what changed and why.
- **Clarity**: Clear. Date, time, and reason included.
- **Tone**: Polite and professional. 
- **Completeness**: Good. Includes a call to action.
- **Score**: 9/10

### AI Response B
"Meeting moved to Wednesday 10am. Monday no longer works."

**Evaluation:**
- **Accuracy**: Correct but too brief.
- **Clarity**: Missing context and greeting.
- **Tone**: Rude and unprofessional.
- **Helpfulness**: Low. Does not ask if new time works.
- **Score**: 4/10

### **Final Verdict**
**Response A is preferred.** It is polite, clear, and professional. Response B lacks tone and details needed for client communication.
