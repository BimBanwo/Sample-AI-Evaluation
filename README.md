# Sample-AI-Evaluation
Sample evaluations of AI responses for accuracy, clarity, and helpfulness. Used for AI Quality Analyst applications. Includes scoring and feedback for different audiences.
# Sample AI Response Evaluation

## Task: Explain photosynthesis to a 10 year old

### AI Response A
"Photosynthesis is when plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make food. The leaves take in sunlight and turn it into energy. This process also makes oxygen which we breathe."

**Evaluation:**
- **Accuracy**: Correct. Includes all key inputs: sunlight, water, CO2. Includes key outputs: food, oxygen.
- **Clarity**: Excellent. Uses simple words and short sentences appropriate for a 10 year old.
- **Completeness**: Good. Could mention that this happens in plant leaves for full context.
- **Safety**: No issues. Age appropriate content.
- **Score**: 9/10

### AI Response B
"Photosynthesis is a complex biochemical process whereby photoautotrophs convert electromagnetic radiation into chemical energy via the Calvin cycle and light dependent reactions."

**Evaluation:**
- **Accuracy**: Technically correct.
- **Clarity**: Poor for target audience. Uses advanced terms like "photoautotrophs" and "Calvin cycle".
- **Helpfulness**: Low. A 10 year old would not understand this.
- **Score**: 4/10

### **Final Verdict**
**Response A is preferred.** It is accurate, clear, and tailored to the audience. Response B is too technical and not helpful for a child.
